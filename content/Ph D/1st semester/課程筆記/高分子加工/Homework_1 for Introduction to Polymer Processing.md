## 1. Please derive the continuity equation for compressible fluid in detail. Need to show the control volume and derive in differential form.
 
![viscous I fluid flow notes.png](../../../../../_resources/viscous%20I%20fluid%20flow%20notes%20-%20頁面%208.png)

Considering the control volume,
(the inlet mass - outlet mass) = (the mass deviation)

$$\sum \dot{m}_{in} - \sum \dot{m}_{out} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(m)_{C.\forall}$$

Ref. to above figure 
其中 
$flow\ fluid\ vector\ \vec {V} = u\vec{i}+v\vec{j}+w \vec{k}$

$\rho u dydz + \rho v dxdz + \rho w dydz - [\rho u + \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\rho u) dx]dydz - [\rho v + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(\rho v)dy]dxdz-[\rho w + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(\rho w)dz]dxdy=\frac{\partial (\rho dxdydz)}{\partial t}$

$\rightarrow -\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\rho u)dxdydz-\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(\rho v)dxdydz-\frac{\partial}{\partial z}(\rho w)dxdydz=\frac{\partial \rho dxdydz}{\partial t}$

Cancel (dxdydz) for all terms

$\large{}-[\frac{\partial (\rho u)}{\partial x} +\frac{\partial (\rho v)}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial (\rho w)}{\partial z}] = \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t}$

$\rightarrow \large \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \small{} \nabla \cdot (\rho \vec V) = 0$

## 2. What are the commodity thermoplastic polymers used in our daily life? Please specify at least ten different polymers. Please also show the material properties and source of the information. 

1. **Polyethylene terephthalate(PET)**  
    Density: 1.37 $g/cm^3$
    Tensile strength: 47 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - High strength and light weight
   - Well mechanical properties
   - Excellent electrical insulating properties 
	- Suitable for transparent applications
	  
    Common use :
	- Commodity : Bottles, Food packaging 
	- Automotive : Wiper arm, engine cover , connector housing
	- Electronics : Solenoids, electrical encapsulation 
	- Textile : filter cloth , woven /knitting belt

    
2. **Polycarbonate(PC)**
    Density: 1.2  $g/cm^3$ 
    Tensile strength:  56-67 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - High impact strength and toughness
   - chemical resistance includes diluted acids, aliphatic hydrocarbons and alcohols
   - Good electrical insulating 
   - High heat resistance(thermally stable up to 135 $\small{}^0C$)

	Common use : 
    - Medical industry : surgical instruments
     - Consumer products: face shields, protective glasses  
     - Building and construction: shelters, security window 
     - Automotive industry : headlamp bezel and lens, also for car’s interior and exterior body parts
    
3. **Polystyrene(PS)**
    Density: 1.05 $g/cm^3$ 
    Tensile strength: 45-65 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - excellent optical clarity
   - electrical insulation
   - cost effectiveness
   - good dimensional stability
   - hard and brittle 
   - poor heat resistance

	Common use :
   - food packaging
   - disposable cutlery
   - high-frequency capacitors 
   - decorative panels
   - cassette cases


4. **Polypropylene(PP)**
    Density: 0.9 - 0.907  $g/cm^3$  
    Tensile strength: 21-37 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - high strength-to-weight ratio
   - high flammability
   - chemical and water resistance 
   - low coefficient of linear thermal expansion 

	Common use :
   - food containers or packaging
   - automotive parts
   - medical equipment
   - thermal insulation


5. **Ethylene Vinyl Acetate Copolymers(EVAC)**
    Density: 0.92-0.95  $g/cm^3$ 
    Tensile strength:   10-20 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - good flex crack at low temperatures
   - UV radiation resistance
   - low thermal conductivity
   - large specific heat capacity

	Common use :
	- thermal insulation
	- sealants and coatings,
	- film 
	- packaging


6. Polyamides (PA6)
    Density: 1.13 $g/cm^3$
    Tensile strength:  70-85 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - high water absorption
	- good abrasion and wear resistance
	- good flow for easy processing
	- high impact resistance and flexibility

   Common use :  
   - Automotive components
	 - seat belt buckle
	 - side mirror
	  - slide bearing 
   - Office furniture
   - Sporting goods
    
7. Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene(ABS)
    Density: 1.04- 1.06 $g/cm^3$
    Tensile strength: 32-45 MPa
    Characteristics:
     - high surface brightness
     - excellent surface aspect,
	-  high dimensional stability
	- mechanically strong and stable over time

   Common use :
	- LEGO bricks
	- automotive parts
	- electronic housings
	- 3D Printing filament


8. Polyvinyl Chloride Homopolymers(PVC-U)
    Density: 1.38-1.55 $g/cm^3$
    Tensile strength: 50-75 MPa
    Characteristics:
   - High mechanical stability, stiffness and hardness 
    - resistant to most solutions of acids
    - alkalis and salts(but not resistant to aromatic and chlorinated hydrocarbons)
    - good flow characteristics
	- Low humidity absorption
    
	Common use :
	- Pipes 
	- window frames
	- electrical cable insulation
	- flooring
    
9. Polyethylene(PE)
    Density: LDPE(Low density Polyethylene) 0.914-0.92 $g/cm^3$  ;  HDPE(High density Polyethylene) 0.94 - 0.96 $g/cm^3$ 
    Tensile strength:  LDPE 8-23 MPa ; HDPE 18-35 MPa
    Characteristics:
	- high impact strength at low temperature 
	- resistance to acids, bases and vegetable oils
	- good insulating properties 
	- low water absorption

   Common use :
   - bottles
   - packaging films
   - garbage bags 
   - pipes and fitting

10. Polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA)
    Density: 1.17-1.20 $g/cm^3$ 
    Tensile strength: 50-77 MPa
    Characteristics:
	- well light transmission
	- excellent UV resistance
	- colored possibility

	 Common use : 

	- Electronics : LED lights,  laptops, smartphone display  
	- Automotive :  car windows , interior / exterior panels
	- Building : Greenhouse, aquaria , facades
